# 📱 UdharCard Merchant App

Welcome to the **UdharCard Merchant App** — a powerful digital ledger solution designed specifically for merchants to seamlessly manage customer credit transactions (Udhar) and collections.

> **✨ Design by Rakebig Services ✨**

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 🔐 1. Secure Merchant Authentication
- **Fast Login**: Mobile OTP login via Firebase SMS OTP or Custom SMS Gateway.
- **Email Access**: Standard secure login with password hashing.
- **KYC Verification**: In-app submission of shop details, license, and PAN/Aadhaar cards for admin approval.

### 👥 2. Comprehensive Customer Management
- **Smart Directory**: Search, filter, and track all your customers (e.g., Active, Outstanding Balances).
- **Detailed Profiles**: View customer information, transaction history, and current balances instantly.
- **Credit Limit Control**: Define maximum credit limits per customer to prevent over-borrowing.

### 📒 3. Advanced Udhar Ledger System
- **Real-Time Tracking**: Record "Udhar Diya" (Credit Given) and "Payment Received" (Debit) instantly.
- **Live Ledger Timeline**: Chronological feed of transactions with automatic running balance calculations.
- **Due Date Management**: Set payment deadlines for credit entries.

### 📊 4. Insightful Merchant Dashboard
- **Financial Overview**: View Total Credit Given, Total Amount Received, and Pending Collections at a glance.
- **Active Metrics**: Track active customers over a 30-day period.
- **Interactive Reports**: Visual graphs showing credit versus collections for daily and monthly analysis.

### 💳 5. Integrated QR Payment System
- **Static & Dynamic QR**: Generate general merchant QRs or unique dynamic QRs embedded with specific amounts and remarks.
- **Easy Sharing**: Share payment links directly via WhatsApp, SMS, or native share.
- **Auto-Reconciliation**: Automatic ledger updates upon successful payment gateway webhooks (Razorpay/PhonePe).

### 🔔 6. Automated Notifications
- **Payment Reminders**: Send reminders manually or automatically via WhatsApp API, SMS, or Push notifications.
- **Real-Time Alerts**: Receive instant updates when a customer makes an online payment.

### 📑 7. Extensive Reporting
- **Ledger Statements**: Export complete ledger statements in PDF or Excel formats.
- **Outstanding Balances**: Comprehensive summary of all pending customer balances.
- **Collection Breakdown**: Detailed daily and monthly financial reports for cash vs. online entries.
